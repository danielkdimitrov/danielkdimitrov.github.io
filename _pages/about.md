Welcome to my personal page. I'm a post-doctoral researcher at the [Research Centre for Longevity Risk](https://rclr.nl/) at the University of Amsterdam. My research covers asset pricing, macro-finance, climate risk, financial stability, and risk management.

**Contact:** [daniel.k.dimitrov@gmail.com](mailto:daniel.k.dimitrov@gmail.com)

> _This page shares my ongoing research projects. The views expressed here do not necessarily correspond to those of the central bank._
